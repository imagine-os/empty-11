### Added — PAP-279: shared filter and condition grammar

- `@paperos/core/filter`: Zod 4 `FilterTree` (`and`/`or`/`not` groups, 15 operators over 10 field
  types, `FieldSchema`, `{ $var }` variables, depth 8 / 200 conditions, `v: 1`), `toSql` to a
  Drizzle `SQL` fragment, three-valued `evaluate`, `normalize`, `explain` (en/es),
  `encodeFilter`/`decodeFilter`, `createFilterGrammar` extension hook.
- 500-case fast-check equivalence test between `toSql` on PGlite and `evaluate`; type tests; perf
  test (50 conditions under 1 ms). Demo: `pnpm --filter @paperos/core example:filter`.
- Paths: `packages/core/src/filter/`, `packages/core/examples/filter.ts`, `docs/platform/filter.md`.
- ADR: [0012-filter-grammar](../../adr/0012-filter-grammar.md)
