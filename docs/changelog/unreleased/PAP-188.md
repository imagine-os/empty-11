### Added — PAP-188: open-source CRM and marketing stack survey and decision

- Survey of Twenty, Postiz, Listmonk, Dub, Chatwoot, Umami and PostHog: licence tier, embed / fork /
  reimplement shapes with hour estimates, data-model fit with the PAP-187 CRM entities, API,
  multi-tenancy, self-host cost and maintenance, plus deliverability (Resend, Postmark, SES),
  analytics options and social OAuth review lead times, all with dated citations.
- Decision: build CRM, social queue, broadcasts, support inbox, short links and analytics on the
  tables engine; borrow Twenty's object model, Postiz's adapter shapes and Chatwoot's conversation
  model; Resend primary with SES as the volume escape hatch; no AGPL waiver needed for v0.1.
